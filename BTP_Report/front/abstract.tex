\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This report presents the design and development of CIPD 360 ERP, a scalable academic management
system developed for the Centre for Intelligent Product Development (CiPD) program at IIIT Delhi.
The system aims to address inefficiencies in traditional academic workflows by providing an
integrated platform for scheduling, attendance tracking, feedback collection, and reporting.

A key innovation of the system is the implementation of a WiFi based automated attendance
mechanism, which leverages network level device detection to infer student presence without
requiring manual input or additional hardware. This approach was selected after evaluating multiple
alternatives, including Bluetooth based detection, RFID systems, and face recognition techniques,
with a focus on scalability and practicality.

The system further incorporates a session driven feedback mechanism, enabling structured and
timely collection of student feedback, along with analytics to derive actionable insights. A role based
architecture ensures that administrators, faculty, and students have access to personalized
dashboards and relevant functionalities.

In addition, the platform integrates scheduling, attendance, and feedback into a unified workflow,
allowing automation of key processes such as attendance computation, feedback rollout, and
notification generation. This reduces administrative overhead and enhances operational efficiency.

The proposed system demonstrates the feasibility of building a centralized, data driven academic ERP
tailored for dynamic educational environments, with potential for scalability and adoption across
similar programs.

\vspace{0.4cm}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Academic ERP, Attendance Automation, WiFi Detection, Feedback Analytics, Scheduling
System, Educational Technology
